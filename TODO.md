## Loan Tracker — pooled portfolio Total XIRR

Done as of 2026-08-13, once CIF corpus data (needed to build the terminal cashflow) landed for Araria.

- **Scope**: pooled per CLF, **active loans only** — LokOS drops a closed loan's origination metadata (amount, disbursement date) once it closes, so a closed loan's outflow can never be reconstructed; including its repayment inflows without a matching outflow would inflate the rate artificially.
- **Realized XIRR (no terminal value) was tried and dropped**, not shipped: for an active, mid-tenure loan book, most disbursed principal is legitimately still outstanding (not overdue, just not due yet), and Realized XIRR gives that zero credit — it came out near-uniformly around -85% across all 53 Araria CLFs regardless of actual repayment behaviour (even restricting to only recently-disbursed loans made it worse, -87% to -99.97%, since those have had even less time to repay anything). It was measuring "this book is young," not CLF performance, so it isn't a useful comparative signal here.
- **Total XIRR is the only figure shipped**: real disbursement/repayment cash flows (dated) + the currently-outstanding balance credited back as one final inflow, dated today. Came out plausible: -17% to +28% across CLFs, median -0.37%.
- CIF corpus (State → CLF) is **not** part of the XIRR cash flow series — it's capital funding the lending activity, not a return on it. It's used only for the separate Active Lending Turnover KPI above.
- Repayment-log truncation (starts ~Oct/Dec 2024, varies by district) still applies — loans disbursed earlier with real repayments before the log start will understate Total XIRR.
